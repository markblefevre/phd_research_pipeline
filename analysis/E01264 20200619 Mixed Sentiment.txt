Why LMMD could still come out slightly positive

A dictionary score like lmmd_net can end up slightly positive here for a few reasons.

1. The negative tone is highly contextual and multi-clause

A lot of the bad news is expressed through longer constructions like:

demand slowdown

worsening environment

impairment tied to structural reform

profit deterioration relative to prior year

difficult achievement of targets

A lexicon may miss some of that if the exact negative meaning comes from the full sentence rather than isolated negative tokens.

2. There is a lot of neutral/managerial/aspirational language

The MD&A also contains many “corporate positive” phrases such as:

持続的な成長

企業価値の向上

成長戦略の推進

収益拡大

強化

着実な遂行

拡大に努めた

改善

回復

A dictionary can count these positive-looking words even when the overall paragraph is bad news.

3. Segment discussion is mixed, not uniformly negative

The engineering segment is actually described positively:

増収

増益

着実な遂行

運営型事業の拡大

So if LMMD is aggregating token counts across the full MD&A, positive wording from one segment can offset severe negativity elsewhere.

4. Finance dictionaries can underweight magnitude

A human or LLM sees this as very negative because of the combination of:

huge earnings deterioration

impairment

net loss

debt pressure

target failure

restructuring

But lexicon scoring often reduces all that to token counts. If the text contains enough positive/neutral managerial vocabulary, the net score can drift positive even though the economic message is clearly bad.
